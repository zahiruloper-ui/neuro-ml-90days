### Key Takeaways from Quiz
1. Standardization is essential for fair feature contribution in PCA and k-means.
2. Explained variance ratio tells you how much info each PC captures.
3. Degenerate clustering (one huge cluster) = continuous data, not discrete groups.
4. Elbow method is a heuristic, not a truth detector.
5. PCA visualization validates clustering results (or lack thereof).
6. Clusters ≠ biological truth — always use cautious language.
7. Time samples ≠ independent subjects — clustering correlations, not people.
8. Dimensionality reduction (PCA) speeds up clustering and removes noise.
